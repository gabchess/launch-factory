# Blog post

## Reader

A sales manager who is deciding whether this change is worth their time. They know the product, they skim, and they want to know what is different for their team.

## Parts

Write these parts in this order, one line each unless noted.

1. Title. Plain, states the change, no hype.
2. Story. One or two lines on the problem a user had before this change.
3. Change. Two or three lines on what is new and what the user can now do.
4. Proof. One line with a number or an example, labelled with where it comes from, such as "measured in the beta" or "from the release notes".
5. Caveat. One line on what the change does not do yet, or a limit the user should know.
6. Status and next step. One line that says whether it is released, in beta or planned, and what the reader can do next.

Put a source marker such as [1] after every claim in parts 2 to 6. The title carries no marker.

## Length

7 to 12 lines in total. 60 to 250 words in total. The title counts as a line. Cut a part before you pad one.

## Sources

Mark each claim in the text with [1], [2] and so on. After the post, add a "Sources" list with one entry per marker. Each entry is the link the user gave, or "pasted text" when the user pasted it in. Every link the user gave appears in the list, or appears as "not used, because <reason>". Claims you could not trace to any source go under a "Not traced" heading after the list, one per line, and stay out of the post text.
