# FAQ update

## Reader

A customer, or a support agent who answers customers. They arrive with a question about how to do something and want a direct answer.

## Parts

Write 2 to 4 pairs. Each pair has two parts, in this order.

1. Question. A heading that starts with "Q:" and is worded the way a customer would ask it.
2. Answer. One to three short sentences that say how to do it, with a source marker such as [1] after every claim.

Write an answer only when a source supports it. If a topic is still an open question in the sources, leave it out of the answers and list it under "Not traced". The question line carries no marker.

## Length

4 to 8 lines in total. 16 to 160 words in total. Each answer stays under 40 words.

## Sources

Mark each claim in the answers with [1], [2] and so on. After the last pair, add a "Sources" list with one entry per marker. Each entry is the link the user gave, or "pasted text" when the user pasted it in. Every link the user gave appears in the list, or appears as "not used, because <reason>". Claims and open questions you could not trace to any source go under a "Not traced" heading after the list, and stay out of the answers.
