# FAQ update

## Reader

The audience the briefing names. If the briefing names none, write for a general user of the product and ask one question at the end of the turn: "Who is this for?" Whoever it is, they arrive with a question about how to do something and want a direct answer.

## Parts

Write 2 to 4 pairs. Each pair has two parts, in this order.

1. Question. A heading that starts with "Q:" and is worded the way a customer would ask it.
2. Answer. One to three short sentences that say how to do it, with a source marker such as [1] after every claim.

Write an answer only when a source supports it. If a topic is still an open question in the sources, leave it out of the answers and list it under "Not traced". If the sources support fewer than 2 pairs, write the pairs they support, even one, and the minimums below do not apply. The question line carries no marker.

## Length

4 to 8 lines in total. 16 to 160 words in total. Each answer stays under 40 words. Markers, the Sources list and the Not traced list are not counted.

## Sources

Mark each claim in the answers with [1], [2] and so on. After the last pair, add a "Sources" list with one entry per marker. Each entry is the link the user gave, or "pasted text: <first five words>" when the user pasted it in. Every link the user gave appears in the list, or appears as "not used, because <reason>". Claims and open questions you could not trace to any source go under a "Not traced" heading after the list, and stay out of the answers.
