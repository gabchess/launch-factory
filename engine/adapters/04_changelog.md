## Draft

### TaskLink, tasks linked to social posts

Approval queues often show a task that only says “Approve the next post.” Finding the real post and context can take longer than the decision itself.

**What’s new**
- In TaskLink, the task and social posts are linked.
- Opening a TaskLink task shows the actual post content, not a card describing it.
- Approvers can see what needs a decision instead of hunting for the post first.
