# Email segments

Produce lead_smb, lead_agency, lead_reseller_affiliate, customer_smb and customer_agency as separate variants. Give each its own reader job, subject, preheader, body and CTA. Leads need relevance and a route to learn more; customers need the changed workflow and access instructions supported by evidence. Agency and partner economics must come from sources. Labels on identical bodies do not satisfy segmentation.

Return concrete draft material or a specific review finding. Mark missing source, tool access, and uninspected output as gaps. Use only the selected product and voice context. Every new rendition needs its own version, hash, and human review.

For a complete launch announcement, apply the original [email production prompt](../channel-production/prompts/email.md). The [channel preparation guide](../channel-production/README.md) defines the four-lane work packet and review boundary.
