# Social video

Start from the viewer situation and the demonstrated user action. Plan a truthful opening, short causal sequence, source footage, exact spoken copy, caption treatment and end action. Specify shot purpose and duration separately from camera and performance. Treat a customer-like perspective as creative direction, not evidence that a real customer said or experienced it. Return a cut plan and render request; the operator executes only the authorized rendition.

For a film with actors, read [the one-rendition production protocol](../video-production/PROTOCOL.md) and its prompt templates before preparing the shot request. It carries the exact story/format decision, named references, whole-film audio brief and one-sample job contract. Preserve accepted scripts and sketches; a dry first sample does not erase the film's music, ambience, SFX or caption plan.

For an actor story that reveals an authored app, load [UGC App Reveal](../video-production/recipes/ugc-app-reveal/README.md), recipe ID `ugc-app-reveal`. Use its five named prompts and metadata through this same video lead. Preserve existing actor performances, actual source-code/provider attribution, measured speech/UI timing, final sound cues and exact CTA. A current product-vision brief controls visible copy; its companion records distinguish intended behavior from executed actions.

## Clip specification

Begin inside the viewer's task. Give each beat one visible action and one source-supported point. A person reference controls identity; a product reference controls the interface or object. Keep identity, outfit and setting stable across shots unless an approved change calls for a new version.

Specify these fields separately: clip ID/revision, audience/job, target duration/aspect ratio, source IDs, claim IDs, example status, identity reference, product reference, framing, camera movement, performer action, exact spoken text, speech SHA-256, voice/accent direction, authored text, disclosure text/placement, script/sketch decision references, capability receipt, cost ceiling, provider job ID, output artifact/version/hash and inspection result. Runtime IDs stay unresolved until the proper tool returns them. Credentials never enter this record.

Speech-led shots need exact words and inspected voice/lip sync. A silent insert uses its own action specification. Authored layers or verified captures carry product text, prices and interface controls; a generative actor does not establish product proof. Use a disclosed fictional role or consented real testimony. Exclude fabricated customer results, simulated news authority and claims of guaranteed reach.

## Work through the gates

Source and rights review precede exact script review. Get the intended frame/sketch accepted, then verify the selected provider, supported controls and cost before producing one authorized sample. Show each rendition to the actual human by default. A separate instruction may authorize the protocol's named-film continuous pass with one assembled review; keep unviewed takes pending and preserve that instruction separately from the per-generation job checker. Preserve speech timing when editing captions and dependent layers. No automatic paid retry follows rejection or failure.

Inspect the encoded deliverable for opening clarity, source-supported action, identity/speech fidelity, readable product details, natural pauses, continuity, timing and visible disclosures. Cite frames or timestamps. Preserve editable sources. A full decode, successful render or model score cannot stand in for a human watch.

Before a disputed visual defect becomes a blocking finding, identify the exact artifact hash and frame or timestamp, then inspect a freshly decoded frame from that artifact. Distinguish what the displayed image appeared to show from what the decoded evidence establishes. Retract or narrow a finding when the decode contradicts it.

For render, review or revision work, read [short motion finishing](../finishing/SKILL.md) for kinetic type, exact music/SFX cues, speech Caption JSON and actual provider lineage. Preserve accepted story decisions during an authorized finishing revision. New UGC script/sketch approval remains separate.

The v2 social-video builder supports 1–30 seconds. Separate production recipes can use another duration within their own limits and approved brief. Choose channel crops after inspecting disclosure and caption survival. Every exported rendition has its own hash and human decision.
