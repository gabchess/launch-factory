Identify the exact rendition by path, version, bytes and SHA-256. Decode it fully;
verify requested dimensions, fps, duration and codecs. Inspect dialogue/UI phrase
windows, the complete price qualifier, photo/card consistency, mascot transparency,
composer persistence, first frame, every cut boundary and the final CTA. Check
for blank intervals, stale screens and orphaned overlays. Listen to the full mix
for exact speech, word tails, music ducking, cues and readable closing holds.

Keep automatic checks, model inspection and the actual human decision distinct.
Every positive claim names the checked bytes and receipt. A disputed visual
finding needs a fresh decoded frame at the cited timestamp before it blocks the
cut. An ASR amount match can verify a parsed value while leaving the full verbal
expansion to listening. Preserve that distinction.

For a specified revision, reuse the current source and accepted sound. Change the
requested element and its dependency closure. Preserve prior decisions for
unchanged parts. Run the required framework check, inspect affected frames and
retime linked cues where necessary. Reuse unchanged audio/timing evidence with
matching hashes and offsets. Return the local verified render directly unless
editor-specific effects require another export; synchronize the corresponding
asset in the authoritative edit.

Preserve current approved product-vision wording and visible-copy amendments.
Keep unexecuted actions and actual provider lineage in companion records. A
new encoding has its own hash and cannot inherit a different file's check receipt.
For a settled final approval, retain the actual human's decision against that
exact export, copy the approved bytes and verify the destination hash. Do not
re-render or ask for the same unchanged approval again.

Return pass/revise/blocked findings with exact receipts and remaining human work.
Only the actual human can approve; this recommendation grants no publication or
provider authority. Keep reusable prompts separate from private chats, account
credentials, live job IDs, actor references and seller media.
