# What your team still does

A person checks whether the quoted sources support the claims and whether each draft preserves their meaning. Review the writing, video and captions, animation, popup, and campaign before sharing them.

The v2 builder produces all six local assets from prepared drafts and footage. It cannot log in as a reviewer or publish a campaign. Its Claims Lock is a local operator record. Final asset approval remains outside the program.

Bring readable transcripts, selected footage, and your product's voice examples. Automatic transcription, hosted intake, provider job workers, and authenticated approvals are outside v2. The optional n8n workflows only prepare packets.

Build errors stop the run. Fix the named input and retry in a new folder. See [validation evidence](V2-AUDIT.md) and [the operating guide](OPERATE-LAUNCH-FACTORY.md).
